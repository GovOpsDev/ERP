# Finance upstream analysis — PARTIAL

Evidence inspected locally: OCA/account-budgeting 15.0 commit `b447bab1678f8bea86bb0a6fd92eaa23b5f0390c`, `budget_control` and `budget_control_purchase`; the Odoo 20 Community image used by the isolated Organization stack. These OCA files are individual source evidence, not the reconstructed NSTDA multi-repository merge. Other NSTDA customization remains to be traced.

* OCA `budget_control/models/account_move.py` recalculates invoice-line budget moves on transitions to posted, draft and cancel and checks selected move types after `action_post`; `_valid_commit_state` in `account_move_line.py` is posted. A draft has no actual commitment. Payment is not the actual trigger in the inspected code.
* Invoice line amount before optional tax handling is sign × unit price × quantity × (1 − discount/100); `in_refund` uses a negative sign. Company settings can include all or selected taxes; absent inclusion, the tax computation uses excluded totals. The generic mixin converts foreign currency to company currency at the commit date.
* In `budget_control_purchase/models/account_move_line.py`, invoice quantity is converted from invoice UoM to PO UoM, then limited to `min(invoice_qty, purchase_qty − (qty_invoiced − invoice_qty))`. For a positive quantity and an existing PO commitment, an invoice calls PO `commit_budget(reverse=True)` and a refund calls it with `reverse=False`. The default PO amount is PO `price_unit × converted quantity`, with configured tax logic, rather than the invoice subtotal. An optional `return_amount_commit` override changes the invoice reversal amount. Forward-commit cases may use another analytic account. Thus 10 units / 100 PO and a same-price, untaxed bill for 4 normally imply 40 actual and 60 remaining PO commitment; a differently priced bill creates a different actual while reducing commitment at PO price. Refund behavior is conditional on a linked PO with `amount_commit`, positive computed quantity and forward/return hooks; do not infer that every credit note restores commitment.
* OCA recomputes existing moves by unlinking and rebuilding them. The current target uses an immutable signed ledger; preserving the business outcome requires reconciliation and explicit reversals instead of copying those deletes.

Odoo 20 `account.move._post(soft=True)` can defer future documents; only records actually posted may produce Budget actuals. `action_post` calls `_post(soft=False)`; native `button_draft` and `button_cancel` perform accounting checks and change state. Purchase adds `account.move.line.purchase_line_id` and explicitly copies it in `_copy_data_extend_business_fields`; PO lines expose `invoice_lines`. Purchase's `qty_invoiced` includes non-cancelled draft invoices, so it cannot be used directly as posted-only Budget consumption. Access to all linked invoice lines must be verified before reconciling, including hidden records. Actual amount and commitment amount need separate source identities and cross-key locking.

The current Procurement subset already rejects taxes, foreign-currency POs and multiple analytic combinations. The Finance adapter implements the corresponding fail-closed slice; full refund and price variance are tested within it. Currency conversion, included taxes, forwarding and account/analytic overrides remain unimplemented or unverified.

The upstream test `budget_control_purchase/tests/test_budget_purchase.py::test_02_budget_purchase_to_invoice` verifies an untaxed 3 × 10 PO, a one-unit bill (PO 20, actual 10), and bill cancellation (PO 30, actual 0). It does not establish refund behavior. The separate `budget_allocation_purchase_deposit` override treats deposits specially, including zero or negative quantities; deposits are outside the validated subset.

Budget reconciliation now isolates PR/PO move types from actuals when invoked by Procurement, and the Finance adapter supplies actual targets when it reconciles a supported linked PO. A regression test verifies actuals are retained. The supported boundary and remaining gaps are described in `finance-budget-lifecycle-v1.md`.
