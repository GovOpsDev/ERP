# Source document amount policy

Required source: `account-budgeting/budget_control/models/base_budget_move.py` (`_budget_include_tax`, `_get_amount_convert_currency`), company all/selected tax lists; `budgeting/budget_control_purchase_manual_currency` supplies a document budget rate. Purchase forecast uses PO money/taxes; accounting actual includes invoice discount; Contract forecast uses journal currency and has no forecast tax list.

The target normalizes new commitment amounts to company currency. Company settings explicitly choose net, all taxes or selected taxes independently for purchase/account/expense. Compatibility mode preserves historical target constraints until an administrator selects a source policy. Native full tax computation supplies the price-included base before selecting tax components, including negative WHT. This avoids treating an excluded price-included tax as part of the base. Only Budget/Accounting managers may set a manual budget rate; ordinary native rates remain available to document users. Company and tax scope are checked.

PO approval captures company unit cost, tax components and currency rate; later tax/rate/config changes do not revalue remaining commitment. Contract captures forecast unit cost and original journal currency. Old confirmed documents without a snapshot retain the company-currency historical basis; no old ledger, approval or audit date is backfilled. Actual uses the native posted expense balance and native invoice rate for selected tax components, captures its resulting policy and amount, and retains each immutable ledger node's snapshot on reversal. Native GL remains authoritative for FX variance; PO rate is never applied again to actual. Reset/repost creates new current snapshots while old nodes retain their original JSON.

Native posting/reset contexts cannot reclassify or reassign Budget ledger records through `default_*`. Posted financial fields, including tax/payable lines, cannot be edited outside native posting/reset actions. Accounting actions and Budget changes share a savepoint, so an FX variance over budget leaves the bill draft and all original commitment intact.
