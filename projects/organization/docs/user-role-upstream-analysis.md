# User role upstream analysis

Status: source analysis for the Odoo 20 `erp_user_role` implementation. Decisions describe the implemented direction unless explicitly deferred.

NSTDA ERP-Government checkout `5a1722c4c6f6cfdf3ed59362a56e85777c58a383`, `odoo/custom/src/repos.yaml`, pins OCA server-backend base `0a493bf39e3ee6dce4ff4423755868aa09b0c740` plus merge `995ad9e9ef32bc6b7ddddc7a01ddfc3235e8ad18`. These are separate merge inputs, not a claim that the second is a descendant of the first. `addons.yaml` selects both `base_user_role` and `base_user_role_restrict_update`. `egov_install` depends on both; `egov_user_role` supplies domain-specific roles and depends on the latter and numerous business modules. Its presets are out of scope.

| Upstream component | Source and behavior | Required in Odoo 20 | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role model and group relation | `0a493bf:base_user_role/models/role.py`: `res.users.role` delegates to `res.groups` through `group_id`; `implied_ids` are role groups; role contains `line_ids` and computed access/rules. | Reusable role, group mapping and assignments. | REDESIGN: separate company-owned role rather than delegated group. |
| User relation and synchronization | `0a493bf:base_user_role/models/user.py`: `role_line_ids`, computed `role_ids`, default-user role copying, and create/write synchronization. It replaces the user's complete `groups_id` with the union of enabled role groups; force is used on deletion. | Preserve unrelated direct membership and shared groups. | REDESIGN: incremental changes with tracked ownership; do not copy default assignments. |
| Effective period | `role.py`: `res.users.role.line` has `user_id`, `role_id`, `date_from`, `date_to`, computed `is_enabled`; endpoints inclusive, using Python's current date. Unique user/role pair; no company or OU field. | Dated assignments, multiple units and company validation. | REDESIGN: inclusive dates, separate OU-aware assignment. |
| Schedule | `0a493bf:base_user_role/data/ir_cron.xml`: every three hours calls `cron_update_users`. | Reconcile transitions without user edits. | MIGRATE concept, with documented execution delay and Odoo 20 scheduler fields. |
| Implied groups and inspection | `0a493bf:base_user_role/models/res_groups.py` adds role and parent lookup; role collects transitive implied groups and exposes access/rule counts. | Native computed implied memberships. | USE ODOO NATIVE for implication; DEFER access/rule inspection views. |
| Access and company | `0a493bf:base_user_role/security/ir.model.access.csv`: `base.group_erp_manager` CRUD on both models; role and role line have no company/OU field; `_bypass_rules` sudoes selected role operations. | Explicit company scope and restricted delegation. | REDESIGN; no broad sudo as authorization. |
| Views and hooks | `0a493bf:base_user_role/views/{role,user,group}.xml`; role UI has group and user tabs; user form integrates role lines; no module hooks in manifest. | Basic roles and assignment menu. | REDESIGN views; DEFER group/user integration. |
| Restrict update extension | `995ad9e:base_user_role_restrict_update/models/role.py` adds `unrestrict_model_update` and `is_readonly_user`; onchange writes flags to role-line users. Depends on `base_model_restrict_update`. | Not required for generic group synchronization. | DEFER; no corresponding write restrictions are claimed. |
| NSTDA presets | `egov_user_role/data/*.xml`: role-associated groups and business group implications, including cross-OU access groups. | No business presets in this milestone. | DEFER. |

Odoo 20 `odoo/addons/base/models/res_users.py` calls the direct M2M `group_ids` (not upstream Odoo 15 `groups_id`) and computes `all_group_ids` from group closure. `res_groups.py` also exposes the inverse direct M2M `user_ids`; changes to either side must be considered. A group grant is global to the user, so role company and organization fields do not themselves restrict business records to that company or unit.
