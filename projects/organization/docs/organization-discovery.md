## Design and security boundary

`erp_organization` depends only on `erp_core`. An organization unit is a company-owned reference record, optionally nested only inside the same company; codes and names are unique within each company. Once created its company cannot change, to avoid invalidating a hierarchy. Odoo 20's `ir.access` restricts **all** access to active companies. Internal users can read assigned units in their active companies; organization managers may manage units and assignments in active companies. This group does not restrict access to accounting, HR or other records. Departmental links are available through the optional HR bridge; delegation remains unimplemented. Treat this as an initial structure, not NSTDA operating-unit security parity. Verify role design with owners before production.
