# Asset accounting transfer — restricted subset

NSTDA `l10n_th_account_asset_management` depends on `account_asset_transfer`: its transfer extension applies a removed sub-status to source assets after OCA AUC transfer. The inspected OCA 18 transfer accepts multiple open AUC assets with nonzero residual and no planned depreciation (`method_number = 0`), posts a reclassification move and creates draft destination assets, then marks the sources removed. It can split or merge; the standard wizard records a common journal rather than an allocation matrix. The Thai sub-status extension is not yet ported. `account_asset_operating_unit` adds an OU field and report fields; the inspected Thai/OU asset modules do not establish an administrative OU/custodian/location transfer workflow. This is a finding for the inspected source, not proof that no such workflow exists elsewhere.

`erp.asset.asset` remains immutable bill/acceptance acquisition provenance. OCA `account.asset` owns depreciation and accounting lifecycle. `erp.asset.transfer.history` is an immutable event with company, OU, operator, date, posted journal, sources, destinations and value. It links a complete group of sources to destinations without inventing per-source allocation amounts; traverse events to audit lineage. Current operational access is a stored OU on transferred destination assets and the original acquisition OU on source assets, not recursive graph traversal. Original acquisitions are never rewritten.

The transfer manager must have native Accounting rights and access to every source. Sources must be in the same company and active assigned OU, have an ERP scope, satisfy OCA AUC eligibility and have no posted depreciation. Destination profiles must belong to the company and create draft assets. Cross-company, cross-OU, already-depreciated and unscoped transfers are rejected. Each source must have positive value and the transfer date cannot precede source acquisition or accounting entries. Accounting move and destination asset totals must equal source purchase value at company-currency rounding; source rows use ordered locks and version updates to prevent concurrent transfers. Transfer journal, its lines, source removal state and the history cannot be reset or rewritten after history is created. Assets participating in history cannot be deleted. Destination profile accounting fields are protected once scoped.

A supported AUC has no posted depreciation; its source is removed and new draft destinations begin their own OCA depreciation schedule when validated. No accumulated depreciation is moved or restarted for already-depreciated assets because that case is rejected. Partial transfer leaving a live source, cross-OU allocation, currency conversion, government sub-status, physical location/custodian history and reclassification reversal are not supported. OCA numbering uses the accounting move reference; unique government numbers for new descendants have not been implemented. History preserves group-level lineage and amounts, not a precise attribution of each source to each destination. These boundaries require further source and accounting analysis before broad deployment.
